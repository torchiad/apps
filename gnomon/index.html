Times on the slider are UTC, not local clock time — solar noon (when the sun is highest) drifts across the day depending on longitude and the equation of time. Building shadows come from OpenStreetMap footprints (via Overpass), simplified to their outer hull, using each building's tagged height where available or the assumed height above otherwise — real shadows may differ due to roof shape, trees, or nearby terrain. Day/night shading and sun position use standard low‑precision solar formulas.
